## Case File Entry #01 — Deconstructing the Unreliable Mind

Every great psychological thriller operates on a single chilling truth: *the human memory is not a recording device—it is an editor.*

When crafting **The Verma Legacy** and **The Shadow Who Watched**, my primary goal was to construct characters whose internal monologues feel deeply vulnerable, yet dangerously flawed. 

### Key Elements of Psychological Tension:

1. **Isolation as a Narrative Pressure Cooker:** When a character has no external anchor to validate reality, paranoia takes root.
2. **The Fragile Threshold of Trust:** In dark mystery fiction, the enemy is rarely standing in front of you with a weapon—they are sitting across from you at breakfast.
3. **Layered Revelation:** Giving the reader clues 3 pages before the protagonist realizes them creates a breathless sense of impending revelation.
