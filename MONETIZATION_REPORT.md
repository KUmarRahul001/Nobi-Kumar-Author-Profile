# Digital Monetization & Adsterra Placement Policy

**Domain:** `https://authornobikumar.netlify.app`  
**Date:** August 3, 2026

---

## 1. Monetization Governance Rules
- **Primary Business Objective:** Sell books & build author authority.
- **Secondary Objective:** Monetize non-transactional traffic via Adsterra.

---

## 2. Ad Placement Matrix

| Page Route | Allowed Format | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homepage (`/`)** | Display Banner | Allowed | Non-intrusive container below featured books. |
| **Blog Listing & Articles (`/blog/*`)** | Display Banner | Allowed | Appears after article content body. |
| **About Page (`/about`)** | Native Banner | Allowed | Placed at bottom before newsletter signup. |
| **Book Detail Pages (`/books/*`)** | **STRICTLY NONE** | **PROHIBITED** | Preserves buying intent & conversion rate. |
| **Contact & Legal Pages (`/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`)** | **STRICTLY NONE** | **PROHIBITED** | Clean user experience & trust. |

---

## 3. Disallowed Formats
The following formats are permanently disabled to maintain premium brand identity:
- ❌ Popunders / Popups
- ❌ Social Bar / Floating Sticks
- ❌ Interstitials
- ❌ Auto-Redirects / Smartlinks
